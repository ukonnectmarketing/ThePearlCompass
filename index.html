BEFORE YOU COMMIT TO ANYTHING

Buying luxury property on the Spanish coast raises questions most people can't answer alone.

And most of them only surface after the deposit is paid — when it's already too late. These are the ones we make sure you can answer while every option is still open.

01

How do I know I'm not overpaying?

You can't see true market value from the outside. We can, and we tell you before you make an offer.

02

What if something's wrong with the property?

Hidden defects, structural issues, illegal extensions. We uncover what the seller won't mention.

03

Who is actually on my side here?

In Spain, most agents work for the seller, or for both sides at once. We represent you, and no one else — with no conflict of interest, ever.

04

Will there be a legal or tax surprise later?

The paperwork, taxes and ownership structure, understood fully and explained clearly before you sign — never after.

05

What if I don't speak the language?

Every conversation, document and negotiation — handled entirely for you, and made clear in your own language

06

How do I do all this from another country?

One trusted point of contact managing the entire process, so distance never becomes a disadvantage.

This is what representation means: someone on your side of the table, from the first viewing to the final signature.
